# FediShield

**A community-based blocklist for the Fediverse.**

FediShield is a community-based blocklist project for people across the Fediverse. The goal is to help users and instance moderators keep track of domains and individual accounts they may want to block on their own accounts or instances.

Anyone can request that a domain or an individual account be added to the blocklist. Requests can be submitted for review, and the project aims to make it easier for people to share information about accounts and instances they have concerns about.

FediShield is an independent project. Inclusion on this blocklist represents a community submission or moderation decision, not an official verdict from the entire Fediverse.

## What is included?

FediShield can include:

- **Domain blocks:** Requests to block entire Fediverse instances or other relevant domains.
- **Account blocks:** Requests concerning individual accounts on supported Fediverse platforms.
- **Community submissions:** Users can suggest domains and accounts for consideration.
- **Documentation:** Information about entries and the reasons provided for their submission, where available.

### Requesting a Domain Addition

If you want to suggest a domain for FediShield, please use the **GitHub Discussions** page.

1. Visit the [FediShield Discussions](https://github.com/zoeyfediverse/fedishield/discussions) page.
2. Start a new discussion explaining which domain you think should be added.
3. Include any relevant links, evidence, or context to help explain your request.
4. Wait for the request to be reviewed. Submitting a request does not guarantee that the domain will be added.

Please keep discussions respectful and focused on the domain being reported. Avoid posting private information, making unsupported accusations, or using the blocklist to target people over personal disagreements.
## Submission guidelines

To keep FediShield useful and fair to contributors and the people being reported, please follow these guidelines:

- Provide links and specific information whenever possible.
- Explain the reason for your request rather than submitting an unexplained name.
- Include relevant evidence, such as public posts or documented behavior.
- Do not submit fabricated claims, private information, or personal identifying information.
- Avoid submitting accounts solely because of personal disagreements or differences of opinion.
- Do not use this project to encourage harassment, dogpiling, or targeted abuse.
- Respect the privacy and boundaries of everyone involved.

Reports will be considered individually. Not every request will result in an addition, and entries may be revised or removed if new information becomes available.

## Important notes

FediShield is a community-maintained resource, not a universal blocklist for every Fediverse server or user.

Being listed does not automatically mean that an account or domain has been independently verified to have engaged in misconduct. Users and instance administrators should review the available information and decide what moderation actions are appropriate for their own communities.

FediShield does not control how third-party platforms use this information. A listing here does not automatically block an account or domain on your instance.

## Contributing

Community participation is welcome! You can help by:

- Submitting a domain or account for consideration.
- Providing relevant evidence or additional context.
- Reporting outdated or incorrect entries.
- Suggesting improvements to the documentation.
- Helping keep the list organized and up to date.

Please be respectful when participating in discussions and when submitting requests.
